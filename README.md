# Nolla Health

### Personal doctors for everyone.

Nolla Health is building an **AI-powered healthcare system designed to deliver end-to-end care** — from understanding a patient's symptoms to diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, and ongoing follow-up.

On **October 5, 2026**, we launched the first AI-powered initial prescription program in the United States through **Nolla Derm in Utah**.

For the first time, patients can receive an initial prescription generated by our AI within a regulated clinical framework, without first seeing a doctor in person.

This is the first step toward our larger vision:

> **A personal doctor for everyone, powered by AI and guided by real physicians.**

# 🩺 Nolla Derm

Our first autonomous clinical application is **Nolla Derm**.

We started with dermatology because acne is common, well understood, visually assessable, and generally low risk when treated within clearly defined clinical protocols.

Today, eligible Utah residents aged 18+ can use Nolla Derm to:

1. Complete a clinical intake
2. Provide relevant medical information
3. Scan their skin using their phone
4. Have their skin analyzed by our AI
5. Receive a personalized treatment plan
6. Receive an initial prescription when appropriate
7. Have medication delivered or sent to a pharmacy
8. Track their progress over time
9. Contact a licensed physician whenever additional care is needed

# 🤖 AI-Powered Prescribing

Nolla Derm represents a fundamental shift in how AI can participate in healthcare.

Traditional AI healthcare:

```text
Patient
   ↓
AI
   ↓
Recommendation
   ↓
Human clinician
   ↓
Prescription
```

Nolla's initial-prescription system:

```text
Patient
   ↓
AI intake
   ↓
AI clinical assessment
   ↓
Safety / eligibility checks
   ↓
Physician-approved treatment protocols
   ↓
AI-generated prescription
   ↓
Pharmacy
   ↓
Ongoing monitoring
```

The AI does **not** have unrestricted prescribing authority.

It operates within a tightly defined clinical system containing physician-approved treatment pathways, eligibility requirements, hard stops, and escalation mechanisms.

When a patient falls outside the system's defined clinical boundaries, **the AI stops**.

The patient is routed to a licensed physician instead.

# 👨‍⚕️ AI + Physicians

We don't believe the future of healthcare is **AI instead of doctors**.

We believe it is:

> **AI handling what can safely be automated, while physicians focus on what requires human expertise.**

Nolla's system therefore includes physicians throughout the care architecture.

Patients can contact a licensed physician directly through Nolla.

Our initial Utah program also uses staged physician oversight as the system scales.

### Stage 1

The first 100 patients have every AI-generated prescription reviewed and approved by a licensed physician before it reaches the patient.

### Stage 2

As the program expands, prescriptions can be issued directly by the AI while physicians perform retrospective clinical review.

### Stage 3

At scale, physicians review a defined sample of prescriptions as well as cases involving adverse events or escalation.

This creates a continuous feedback loop:

```text
AI
 ↓
Clinical Decision
 ↓
Patient Outcome
 ↓
Physician Review
 ↓
Safety Monitoring
 ↓
System Improvement
 ↓
AI
```

# 🔬 Clinical Evidence

Nolla's AI is built around clinical research, structured protocols, and physician validation.

Our acne severity model has been trained using **more than 100,000 labeled acne cases** and, according to our submitted Utah proposal, achieved an **R² of 0.947** on a held-out test set of 10,348 cases.

In our reported real-world evaluation, licensed clinicians agreed with the AI's treatment recommendations in **more than 96% of cases**, with disagreements involving adjustments such as topical strength rather than fundamentally different treatment pathways.

The objective isn't to create a black-box system that happens to produce plausible answers.

The objective is to create a **measurable, constrained, auditable clinical system**.

# 🏗️ The Nolla Architecture

At a high level, Nolla is built around several interconnected layers:

### 01 — Patient Interface

The patient interacts with Nolla through a simple consumer application.

### 02 — Multimodal AI

The system can reason over different forms of information including:

* text
* medical history
* questionnaires
* images
* longitudinal observations
* health data

### 03 — Clinical Intelligence

AI models transform raw patient information into structured clinical signals.

### 04 — Clinical Protocols

AI decisions are constrained by physician-designed treatment pathways.

### 05 — Prescription Infrastructure

When a patient qualifies, Nolla can connect the treatment decision to prescription and pharmacy workflows.

### 06 — Physician Network

Licensed physicians provide escalation, oversight, and direct patient care.

### 07 — Longitudinal Monitoring

Patient outcomes feed back into the care loop.
